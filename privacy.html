Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Who uses this app

Clio is a private application used only by its owner, Gianluca Volterrani, with accounts that belong to him. It is not available to the public.

What data it accesses

With the owner's explicit consent through Google OAuth, Clio may access Google user data in the owner's accounts (such as Gmail messages, calendar events and Drive files), limited to the permissions shown on the consent screen.

How the data is used

  • Data is used only to perform tasks requested by the owner.
  • Data is not sold, not shared with third parties for their own purposes, and not used for advertising.
  • Some processing may use third-party AI model providers acting on the owner's behalf, only to perform those tasks. Clio does not use Google user data to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.
  • No human other than the owner reads the data, except where required by law.

Clio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored on servers controlled by the owner, protected by access controls and not published anywhere.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions.
